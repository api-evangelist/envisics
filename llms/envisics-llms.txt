## How to call it

- Base URL: `https://envisics.com/wp-json`
- Authentication: none. Send no Authorization header.
- Pagination: `page` + `per_page` (1-100); totals in the `X-WP-Total` and `X-WP-TotalPages` response
  headers; `Link` carries `rel="next"`.
- Trim payloads with `_fields=`; inline relations with `_embed`.
- Errors: `{"code":"...","message":"...","data":{"status":404}}` — the WordPress envelope, NOT RFC
  9457 problem+json.
- Rate limits: none published, none signalled. No `RateLimit-*`, no `Retry-After`. Self-throttle and
  cache.
- Writes: not available anonymously. Collections advertise `Allow: GET`.
